Frequently asked questions

When does the liquid in the vessel go down?

The amount you drank goes down 24 hours after the time you recorded. Even when the level falls, the record itself stays in your history.

The home screen shows how long until the next drop. This is based on the recommended daily intake of sugars; it does not show how much sugar is in your body, or how that changes.

How is the sugar guideline decided?

In simple setup it is calculated from your age, weight and selected region; in detailed setup, sex, height and activity level are added.

The guideline is a standard for all the sugars you take in, including food and sweets, while the app records drinks only. So half of the daily guideline is set aside as the “drinks guideline”, and the rest is left for food.

Neither line is a point where something starts to happen. Japan’s Dietary Reference Intakes (2025) state that no clear threshold has been reported above which obesity, type 2 diabetes or dental caries increases or decreases. It is also not a guarantee of an amount that is safe to drink. You can see the sources under Settings → “About this app” → “Data sources”.

Is the guideline the same for under-18s?

If your region has a standard for minors, that is used; if it does not, the adult calculation applies. Japan has no age-specific standard for sugars, so the same formula based on body size is used.

If you are under 18, please use the app together with a parent or guardian.

My drink is not in the list

  • Find it by barcode — use the barcode button on the record screen.
  • Photograph the label — on the My Drinks registration screen, take a picture of the nutrition label, check the values that were read, and register it.
  • Enter it yourself — type the values from the label on the My Drinks registration screen.

The value that was read does not match the label

Values read from a label can be corrected before they are saved. If the barcode search result is wrong, choose “Register from the label” and enter the values yourself.

If reflections or a curved surface make it hard to read, change the angle and try again. Check not only the numbers but also whether they are “per 100 mL”, “per bottle”, and so on.

The sugar value is not what I expected

The app uses the WHO definition of “free sugars” as its guideline, but for some drinks the value is estimated from carbohydrates. For drinks containing milk, lactose is included as well, so the figure may be higher than the free sugars.

How do I move my records to a new iPhone?

Use the backup stored in iCloud. On the new iPhone, sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account you used before, then open Soda Cap.

Before you switch, check “Back up to iCloud” and “Last synced” in the app’s settings. Do not erase the old device until you have confirmed your records on the new one.

Can I use the app without iCloud?

Turn off “Back up to iCloud” in the app’s settings to stop backing up. A backup that has already been stored is not deleted. Without a backup, records lost by deleting the app or erasing the device cannot be restored.

How do I set up notifications?

Notifications are off by default. Turn each kind on under Settings → “Notifications”, and choose when to receive them with “Hours to notify”.

If notifications do not arrive, also check that they are allowed for Soda Cap in the iPhone Settings app.

The widget says “Finish setup in the app”

Open the app and complete the initial setup. The widget can be added to the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for apps bought on the App Store are requested through Apple’s refund process. Apple decides whether a refund is granted.

Reporting a problem

When you get in touch, please tell us:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version
  • the version of Soda Cap
  • what you were doing when it happened
  • a screenshot showing the problem, if possible

If an image contains personal information or records, please hide them before sending.
