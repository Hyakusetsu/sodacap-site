Soda Cap Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

日本語 · English

Soda Cap works without an account. The developer does not collect the profile or records you enter in the app.

What is stored

Your profile (age, weight, sex, height, activity level, selected region and so on), your drink records, My Drinks, My Cups and your settings are stored on your device. The profile is used to calculate your sugar guideline.

iCloud backup

When iCloud is available and “Back up to iCloud” is on in the app, a backup of the data above is stored in your own iCloud. The developer does not retrieve or view its contents.

The backup is on by default. You can change it with “Back up to iCloud” in the app’s settings. Turning it off does not delete a backup that has already been stored.

Barcode lookup

Barcodes that are not found in the app are looked up in the product database Open Food Facts. The barcode number, the app name and version, and the display language are sent, and your IP address is also visible to them. Your profile and drink records are not sent.

For how that information is handled at the destination, please see the Open Food Facts privacy policy. You can also record a drink without the lookup, by choosing from the list or entering the values yourself.

Use of the camera

The camera is used to read barcodes and nutrition labels. Video and photos are analysed on the device; they are not saved as image files and not sent anywhere.

Using the camera is optional. Even if you turn off camera access for Soda Cap in the iPhone Settings app, you can still choose from the list or enter values yourself.

Advertising and analytics

Soda Cap does not show advertising, does not track your behaviour, and does not collect usage data through third-party analytics tools. It does not access your location.

Keeping and deleting data

Records are not deleted automatically. The amount that drops out of the vessel after 24 hours still remains in your history.

Individual records can be deleted from the history. To reset the profile and records inside the app, use Settings → “About this app” → “Delete all data”. This cannot be undone.

If you delete the app, the iCloud backup remains. For how to manage or delete data in iCloud, please see Apple’s guidance.

Use by children

If you are under 18, please use the app under the supervision of a parent or guardian. How data is handled does not change with age.

Contact and changes

For questions, please get in touch through the support page. The content and email address you send us are used to respond to you.

Changes to this policy will be shown together with the update date on this page.

This app is for recording the sugars in drinks. It is not medical advice and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. For health decisions, please consult a doctor or another qualified professional.
