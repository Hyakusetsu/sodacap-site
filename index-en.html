A serious risk from sugary drinks

Drinking large amounts of sugary drinks over time can lead to ketoacidosis, a dangerous condition, even in people who have never been told they have diabetes. In Japan it is known as soft drink ketosis, or "PET bottle syndrome".

Symptoms such as thirst, passing more urine, weight loss and tiredness can come on suddenly. If it gets worse, nausea, abdominal pain and impaired consciousness can follow.

Worked out from your body

Just enter your age, weight, height and a few other details, and Soda Cap works out a daily sugar guideline from the official standards of each of 30 countries and regions and WHO guidance.

Logging takes three seconds

Choose what you drank and how much. That's it.

You can also log from Control Center and Siri. The Home Screen widget lets you check the current level.

Log any drink

Search for a drink by name, or scan its barcode. Barcodes not in the app are looked up in Open Food Facts, a product database.

If it isn't there, enter the numbers from the nutrition label and Soda Cap works out the sugar for you.

Matched to where you live

The same cola can contain a different amount of sugar depending on the country.

Choose your region, and Soda Cap uses that country's sugar standard and the values of drinks sold there.

30 countries and regions are covered, all taken from official food composition tables and manufacturers' published values. If yours isn't listed, choose the international standard.

See the shape of your day

The timeline shows when you drank what, so the times you tend to drink start to stand out.

A month at a glance

A calendar shows your sugar intake for the whole month.

Each day shows its own container, so the days you stayed under the guideline and the days you went over are visible at once.

Tap a day to see what you drank.

Choose your container and colors

Four containers: plastic bottle, can, glass bottle, and glass.

Eight theme palettes. State is shown by symbol as well as color.

You can also choose the first day of the week and the calendar system (Gregorian, Japanese era, Buddhist, or Minguo).

The Lock Screen can show the container and the time until it next goes down.

Your records stay with you

No account. No ads. No usage analytics.

A copy of your records is kept in your own iCloud, so a new phone picks up where you left off.

Nothing is sent to the developer's servers. You can export your records as CSV.
